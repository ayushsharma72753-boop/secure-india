About Me - Why I Built This As A PWD Person

I am a Person with Disability - Visually Impaired (VI). When I go outside, I cannot see potholes clearly on the road. At night, it becomes even more difficult. Many times I have almost fallen. This is very dangerous not only for people like me with low vision, but also for elderly people, children, and every citizen.

That is why I thought of creating an app where anyone can report a pothole with its size, photo, and live location. And I made it smart -
1. If it is a Water complaint, it will automatically go to Jal Vibhag
2. If it is an Electricity complaint, it will go to Vidyut Vibhag (MPEB)
3. If it is Garbage or Pothole, it will go to Nagar Nigam / PWD Road Dept

I am a PWD, but my vision for India is 100% clear. I want to make India Secure for everyone.
